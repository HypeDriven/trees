# Third-party resources

StarHermit Trees has no bundled third-party runtime JavaScript dependencies.

The stylesheet requests **DM Sans** and **Manrope** from Google Fonts. These fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and remain under their own licenses. Font files are loaded by the browser, not bundled in this repository. The app uses system fallbacks when they are unavailable.

- DM Sans source and license: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans
- Manrope source and license: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope

The optional validation scripts use separately installed `wgpu` and `moderngl` Python packages. They are not distributed with this repository.

Botanical references linked in the app are reference sources. No source photographs or scans are bundled.
